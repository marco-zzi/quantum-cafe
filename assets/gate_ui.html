Welcome, guardian

The Gates of Kvantyus

You are the new guardian of the gates of Kvantyus, home of the Kvantyonis. Travellers from the whole realm queue outside, and some of them are Nobbubys, who must never get in.

Your predecessor left before teaching you the spell that tells them apart. You only have a deck of travellers and a little mana.

1Choose which traits the sentinel will look at. Every trait makes each examination cost more mana.
2Spend your mana to examine travellers: it reveals whether each one is a friend or a foe.
3Train the sentinel on what you found. Then it judges everyone else at the gate, and you cannot overrule it.
